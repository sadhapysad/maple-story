About the Game

MapleStory is a 2D online RPG launched in 2003 — colorful maps, hundreds of monsters, and a progression system that kept millions playing for years. You pick a class, explore towns, grind mobs, level up, and get lost in nostalgia. Simple idea. Endless hours.
